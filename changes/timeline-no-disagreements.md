# The Timeline shows no source disagreements

- The classes are the authority (owner's direction, 2026-10-30). A Timeline event no longer shows "Where the sources differ" in the Telegram app, nor "Where sources disagree" in the Bible Strong Timeline build. The introduction no longer says both sides are shown.
- What is not settled (a date's precision) is still shown, under "Not settled".
- The editorial rules say so for new events, and the Timeline editor no longer offers disagreement fields. Older entries keep their `disagreements` data, unused.
