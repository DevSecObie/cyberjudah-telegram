# Said in Class in the CyberJudah app

- `GET /api/app/teachings?q=&feed=&page=` searches every recording's spoken words (the same `searchTeachings` the site and the Telegram app use) for any verified CyberJudah app sign-in, linked to Telegram or not, like the app's Ask. Results are public passages, cached at the edge for five minutes by the search alone.
