# Captivity period pictures (Assyrian, Babylonian, Persian, Greek, American)

Obie's pictures, each cropped to 640x800, as the cover of the period under each captivity:
- `8-assyrian`: Israelite captives led out of a burning city by an Assyrian commander (Isaiah 10:5).
- `8-babylonian`: the lion with eagle's wings (Daniel 7:4); the glowing heart on its chest removed at Obie's ask.
- `8-persian`: the bear raised up on one side with three ribs in its mouth (Daniel 7:5).
- `8-greek`: the leopard with four heads and four wings (Daniel 7:6).
- `fc-into-the-ships` (first period of the American Captivity): the woman on the scarlet beast with seven crowned heads (Revelation 17:3-6), as Deacon Eythan teaches in Our Hidden History ep. 210; sky and ground extended to fit the card.

The five ids are added to the approved period pictures on the web Timeline and in the app timeline builder; `bot/data/bs-timeline.json` is rebuilt (only those periods' images and the revision change).
