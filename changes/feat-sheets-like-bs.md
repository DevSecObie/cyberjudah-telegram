- Sheets work as Bible Strong's do on the iPhone: no ✕ (a swipe down, a tap outside or Back closes them), Bible Strong's 54 px header with the title on the left and a line under it, its grabber over the top, and its sizes: Font and settings opens at 40% and pulls up to full height, tags at 70%, a note or a link at 45% with the reader still usable behind it, the verse's resources, export and a word study at full height.
- Fonts, Share options and the colour palette open over Font and settings, as in Bible Strong, instead of replacing it.
- Pulling a sheet's content down from its top moves the sheet, and pushing it up grows a sheet that has a taller size, as a native sheet does.
- Books, Go to verse and Version open as bottom sheets, as Bible Strong's selectors do, instead of cards under the header: Books and Version at full height, Books' title centred with "Chapters" under a book's name, Go to verse from Books at half height over the books.
- A dictionary word, a theme, a precept, a tag, a study or a timeline event opened in the app slides up as a sheet over the page it was opened from, as Bible Strong's do on the iPhone: at 45% with that page still usable behind it, pulling up to full height. A Strong's entry or a person opens as a full-height sheet. Closing it goes back to the page, which stays the tab's place.
- In a sheet these screens use Bible Strong's header: the title in 14 px bold with its subtitle under it, a back arrow only when the sheet has a screen before this one.
- Opened from a link outside the app, they are pages, as before.
- The tags sheet is Bible Strong's: "Tags for" the verse, a search box that also creates a tag ("Create …" when nothing matches), the chosen tags as chips under it, and a checkbox row per tag.
- Menus that are not attached to a button (rename, new group, delete) open as Bible Strong's action sheets: a bottom sheet with its title and rows, a text field with its button under it, and no Cancel row; a swipe down or a tap outside cancels.
