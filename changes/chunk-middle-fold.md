- Recordings' captions: a short chunk (under 25 words) in the middle of a recording now joins the next chunk instead of being left out. Before, where captions are sparse its words were in no chunk, so search could not find them (about 129,000 words in 5,295 recordings). The joined chunk opens where the short stretch began only when that is at most 45 seconds earlier; after a longer sparse stretch it keeps its own time. The owner chose this rule (option B) on 2026-11-16. The "captions around a moment" excerpt uses it at once. The search index keeps the old chunks until the `EMBED_CHUNK_MIDDLE` repository variable is set to `fold`: that re-embeds 30,224 chunks and removes 8,744 old ids (about 1% of the index). The embedding job now also receives `EMBED_CHUNK_TAIL`, which it did not before; the middle fold takes effect only with `EMBED_CHUNK_TAIL=whole` set too (otherwise the job warns and keeps the old chunks).
