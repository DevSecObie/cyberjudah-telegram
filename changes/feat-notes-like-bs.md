- Notes follows Bible Strong's notes screen: the filter button (Search, Order, Tags, kept for the next visit), then every note as Bible Strong draws it, where it is and how long ago, its title and the start of its text, its tags and the number of its relations. Each has its ⋮ options: View in Bible, Open in new tab and Remove (which removes its relations too). Long lists draw thirty at a time as you scroll.
