- Ask searches three ways at once and fuses them by rank: the teachings index by meaning, the library by keyword, and now the recorded classes' captions by the words actually said, each caption hit read as about two hundred words around the moment and timed to it. The keyword searches look only for what the question is about ("Who are the twelve tribes today?" searches "twelve tribes"), and the forty best are reranked against the whole question.
- Ask's answers take the shape of the question instead of a set form: no fixed order, sections or word range. Every rule about evidence, doctrine, names and citations stays.
- Ask lab (bot/scripts/ask-lab.mjs, workflow ask-lab on the "ask-lab" label): a pull request's Ask against the one it replaces, on the test questions, measuring search precision, class moments, faithfulness to the sources and how natural the answer reads. Read-only; deploys nothing.
