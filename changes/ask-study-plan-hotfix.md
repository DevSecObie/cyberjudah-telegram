# Ask Deep Study hotfix: answer kept after a failed send-back; parts read their own Scripture

- When an answer is sent back for an open part and the extra round's call to the model fails (for example rate limited or overloaded), the reader now gets the answer already written, finished and saved, on Claude and on the other tool-capable models. Before, the request ended with no answer and nothing saved. A failure before any send-back is raised as before. The failure is logged by status only, and research is recorded as stopped by a limit (`rounds`), not as "not found". On the other models, a conversation too long to answer in after the send-back also keeps the answer.
- A part of a research plan now always has to read the Scripture its own wording names, plus the reader's ranges that meet it. The model's own list of Scripture may only add to that. Before, a part that named all of a chapter could count as covered after reading one verse, if the model listed only that verse or nothing at all.
