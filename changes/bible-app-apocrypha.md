The Bible app (the Bible Strong reader at /app/strong) now offers the whole King James Version of 1611: the fifteen books of the Apocrypha appear between Malachi and Matthew in their 1611 order, with their KJV names, and each opens its own text (Tobit no longer showed 1 Esdras). Book names in the book picker are in English. Ecclesiasticus 44 and the Prayer of Manasses, which the reader refused after a text update, open again.
