# Auto: a pronoun is not a word to define

- Questions that point at something earlier in the conversation or on screen are not quick word definitions or short phrases, at any turn, whatever the quotes or punctuation. Examples: "what does it/this/that mean", "meaning of them", "define 'that'", "what's this", "what's there?". The pronouns are: it, its, this, that, these, those, they, them, their, he, him, his, she, her, here, there. Words that only start like a pronoun ("Hebrew", "theophany", "Ithiel") are still definitions.
- "define", "defined", "defines" and "definition" count as asking what something means. "definition of mercy" is a word definition, like "meaning of mercy".
