- The Bible in the CyberJudah app is now Bible Strong's reader: reading, selecting verses, highlights, notes, tags, bookmarks, Free mode word annotations, the book picker, search in the Bible and every other reader behaviour work exactly as in Bible Strong, with the King James Version and its Apocrypha and CyberJudah's teaching. Chapter links (/read/john/3?v=16) open there, under the app's own bottom bar.
- When you select verses, the Bible shows the sheet from Bible Strong's phone app: highlight colours, one row of actions (Note, Tag, Link, Relation, Bookmark, To study, Focus, Free mode) and Annotate / Study / Share tabs.
- Every precept the classes read with a verse now shows after that verse, marked P, the way Bible Strong shows notes; tapping one opens the class's breakdown of it. The CyberJudah classes are also in the commentary list.
- Inside the Telegram app the Bible behaves as Bible Strong's phone app: the book, version and verse pickers, text display, bookmarks, tags and other choices open as bottom sheets, commentaries and precepts open full screen, and the chapter arrows sit at the bottom. Commentary and bookmark titles name books in English.
- The Bible feels part of the app: it loads in the background and stays open between tabs, so it opens at once where you left off; chapter links move it without reloading; Telegram's back button closes its screens; verse taps buzz; and it shares the app's theme both ways (Telegram's light or dark on Auto). Links in the class teaching stay in the app: a scripture opens in the reader, a class on its notes screen, a recording in the app's player; any other link opens through Telegram.
- The CyberJudah lion shows while the app starts and while the Bible loads, in the app's light or dark colours, instead of a blank screen or a spinner.
