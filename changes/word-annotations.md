Bible Strong's free annotation mode in the reader. Double-tap a word, drag across the text with a mouse, or tap Free mode in the selection sheet: the header turns into a bar with Done, and the Free mode toolbar opens. Select words by tapping, dragging or moving the blue handles, even across verses, then mark them as a highlight, an underline or a hand-drawn circle in any highlight colour, or pick another colour with "+". Tap a mark to select it: change its colour or kind, delete it, or give it a note, tags and relations, which show after its last word. Drawing over marks replaces them, and the trash button erases the marks in the selection, with a confirmation when they carry a note, tags or relations. All your annotations are listed under Menu, Annotations, filtered by colour, tag, style, testament and book, by verse, by date or as a list. Existing word marks stay, now drawn the same way.
