## People: forefathers and descendants in the family graph

The Relationships graph shows a person's **forefathers**: people the KJV names in their line without the generations between. For example, Mattathias is "a priest of the sons of Joarib" (1 Maccabees 2:1). The graph also shows the other side, the forefather's **descendants**. Tapping either one walks the line, just as tapping a father or a son does. They use the graph's existing up and down arrows and chips. Ask's person lookup names them too.

The data comes from cyberjudah PR #631. Each person's record gains `line` and `descendants`. Until that is published, both are simply absent and the graph shows what it shows today.
