- Ask CyberJudah answers more like a leading assistant, in the app, on the web and in Telegram: it works out what a vague or many-part question means and answers every part, follows the conversation ("why?", "who was he?"), says plainly what the library does not cover, keeps the classes' own words instead of softening or paraphrasing them, and never invents a reference, quote, date or name.
- The app's Ask reads the verses a question names in exact King James text before answering, returns three follow-up questions, answers a greeting or thank-you in a sentence, and takes longer questions (up to 1,000 characters).
- The free Ask model is now a stronger one that costs less per token than the earlier default; the daily free budget and every limit are unchanged, and the earlier model stays free.
- The app's Ask brings in the study mode first staged from `codex/bible-strong-staging`: a short research plan across the classes, transcripts and public study resources, answers written as source-checked cards, streaming, request tracing and a report endpoint. It now runs on the stronger free model, and its rules add the classes' own words and the Bishops' and Deacons' precedence. Its plain (non-card) answers use the same answer rules as Telegram and the web.
- Ask knows who taught each class it quotes, from the published class notes: the teacher is shown to the model, a Bishop's or Deacon's relevant teaching is lifted into the answer first, and a teacher is never named for a class whose notes don't name one.
- The daily free-answer cap is $1.00 for production and for staging, as the owner set; answers past Workers AI's free daily allowance are billed up to that cap. A second, stronger free model is admitted for comparison.
- Paying readers' Claude answers can be billed to the owner's own Anthropic console credits (`CLAUDE_BILLING` "anthropic"): once the `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` secret is added it pays, with no Unified Billing fee; until then Cloudflare's Unified Billing stays in place. An emptied console balance hands the answer to the backup. Free readers stay on free models.
- Every verse Ask quotes is checked against the King James text before the answer is sent: a misquote of a short reference is replaced with the real verse, a misquote of a long range is taken out.
- Ask has a test set (`bot/evals/ask-questions.json`, run with `bot/scripts/eval-ask.mjs` against staging): 21 real kinds of questions, checked for an answer, honest citations, word-for-word verses, staying on topic, saying plainly what the library doesn't cover, short greetings, and no model names or softening disclaimers; with time per answer. The owner can fill in an approved answer for each.
- Smart routing when the reader has not chosen a model: in the app, a deep question (why and how, comparisons, several questions, a passage to explain) goes to the stronger free model and the rest to the fast one; for paying readers, a light question (a greeting, a short follow-up, a plain who-or-what) goes to the lighter Claude model, which is faster and costs them less, and the rest to the default. - Approved answers: `bot/data/approved-answers.json` holds answers the owner approves word for word for questions asked again and again. The app screens follow after the release.

- When the money that pays for Claude runs out (the owner's console balance, or Cloudflare's credits), paying readers keep getting the backup model's answers without being charged, and the admins are now told in Telegram, at most once every six hours, so it can be topped up.

## Prepaid Ask credit packs (server side)

- App readers can buy prepaid Ask credit packs ($4.99, $9.99, $19.99) through Apple or Google, tracked by RevenueCat. They are one-time purchases and nothing renews, so nothing is ever charged on a Sabbath, feast or New Moon by itself. Each pack adds its price minus the store's cut, so the owner never pays the fee.
- `POST /api/revenuecat/webhook` credits a pack once per store transaction, and a store refund takes back what is still unspent. It only accepts RevenueCat's authorization header (the `REVENUECAT_WEBHOOK_AUTH` secret). Unknown products, anonymous ids and subscription events are ignored. Sandbox purchases count on staging only.
- The app names a reader's balance to RevenueCat by a keyed hash of their sign-in, never the sign-in itself (`GET /api/app/ask/wallet`).
- With packs open, the app's Ask also offers the Claude models, paid from the reader's own balance at cost, held to the confirm limit. If Claude's money has run out, the free model answers instead and the balance is not touched.
- Everything is switched off (`ASK_PURCHASES: "off"`) until the app's purchase screens ship and the owner turns it on.
