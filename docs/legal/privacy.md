## Privacy Policy

*Last updated November 7, 2026*

CyberJudah is a free Bible study app for iPhone, Android and the web. This page explains what it collects, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and how to delete it.

### Without an account
You can read, search and study without an account. Your highlights, notes, bookmarks, studies, reading history and settings then stay on your device.

### With an account
If you sign in with email, Google or Apple, we keep:
- **Your email address, your name and an account ID**, to sign you in.
- **What you create**: notes, highlights, bookmarks, tags, links and studies, with their earlier versions, so they stay in sync across your devices.

These are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Each account can read only its own data.

### How the app is used
The iPhone and Android apps use Google Firebase Analytics to count which screens and features are used, linked to your account ID when you are signed in. We use this only to improve the app. There is no advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

### Other services
- **Cloudflare** hosts our server and the study library.
- **YouTube** plays the videos. When you play one, YouTube receives your device's request, as it does on its own site.
- **Apple App Attest and Google Play Integrity** confirm that requests come from the real app. They carry no personal information.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else.

### How long it is kept
Account data is kept until you delete it or delete your account.

Technical logs, statistics, backups and data held by providers have retention periods specific to their services and purposes. Information needed for a legal obligation or dispute may need to be retained separately. Contact us for the retention period applicable to a category of data or to request its deletion.

### Delete your data
In the app, go to **More → Delete account**. This deletes your sign-in and your synced data. You can also email privacy@cyberjudah.io. We answer within 30 days.

### Children
CyberJudah is not directed to children under 13.

### Changes
When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
