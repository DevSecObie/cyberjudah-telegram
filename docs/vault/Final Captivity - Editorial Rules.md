# Final Captivity: editorial rules

1. **Never fabricate.** Every fact needs a source. Every teaching quote must match the class, within 60 seconds before to 10 minutes after the cited moment, and the checker enforces this.
2. **The assembly's teaching is its own section**, attributed to the teacher and linked to `https://youtu.be/<id>?t=<sec>`.
3. **Images** are archival (public domain, credited) or clearly labelled as generated.
4. **The owner's IUIC rule:** never record outside characterisations of IUIC or the assembly. That means no designations, labels or accusations from critics, former members or third parties, in events, disagreements, accounts or sources. Never use the SPLC or similar watchdog publications as a source.
5. **The Israel United in Christ period covers leaders and the organization only:** founding, leaders, schools and camps, publications, broadcasts, missions at home and overseas ("show we went there"), and ministries. "None of the haters."
6. Book names follow the app (for example, "Ecclesiasticus").

Removed under rule 4: the SPLC-designation and Joy Morgan drafts, the Kendrick Lamar and Ebenezer events, the 2013 split draft, and the CDAMM characterisation. The three forerunner events now cite israelunite.org/history.
