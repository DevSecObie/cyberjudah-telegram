# Final Captivity: research tools

These are scratch tools from the session. They were not committed and live only in the session's scratchpad (`fc/`).
- `tsearch.py`: find, read and title search over an index of about 7,400 class transcripts.
- `wb.py`: fetches a page, falling back to the Wayback Machine.
- `merge.py`: merges agent batches. Published events win over drafts, and duplicates are removed.
- `coverage_md.py`, `inventory.py`, `years.py`: coverage and year-gap reports.
- `BRIEF.md`: the brief given to research agents (it includes the IUIC rule).

The TranscriptAPI connector can give a video's publish date for 1 credit.
