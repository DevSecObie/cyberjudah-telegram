# Review rules for every portrait (from the KJV, the Apocrypha and the assembly's teaching)

An image PASSES only if ALL hold. When in doubt, FAIL and regenerate.

Frame
- One person, head and shoulders, painted; face whole and centred (a circle crop keeps the whole face).
- Square full-bleed slate-blue background to the edges. A thin light frame is OK (trimmed). FAIL: painted inside a circle/oval, dark or ornate frame, white page.
- No text, letters, numbers, watermark. No halo, glow behind the head, light rays, cross, star, horns, wings, weapons, armour. No nudity, nothing grotesque, deformed or comic. Calm, dignified.
- EVERY FACE MUST BE ITS OWN PERSON: fail if a face looks like the same man or woman as another tile, or as any approved portrait on the reference sheets ref-1.jpg to ref-4.jpg (read them before reviewing). Same-age elders must still differ in face shape, nose, eyes and build.

People (everyone was dark before the mingling, Genesis 2:7; only Esau's line came out red, Genesis 25:25)
- israel, hebrew, adam, ham: clearly Black, dark-brown to deep-black skin, tight woolly coils. Men have a FULL head of hair: FAIL on bald, balding or receding hairlines ("no bald heads", Leviticus 21:5), except Pharaoh (shaven) and Elisha. FAIL on locs/twists, straight or loose wavy hair (grey too must be tight coils), or a light face.
- Adam: in the likeness of Christ, a full thick head of WHITE woolly hair and a full white woolly beard (Revelation 1:14).
- edom (incl. Rome/Italy): fair skin with a natural ruddy flush, thin straight hair. FAIL if Black, or a sunburnt caricature.
- moab, ammon, ishmael, syria, assyria, elam, japheth: DARK-BROWN skin, and the features of their people today: moab Chinese, ammon Japanese, ishmael Arab, syria Syrian, assyria Kurdish, elam East Indian, japheth Polynesian/Pacific Islander. FAIL if the skin is light or the features don't match.
- cainline (Cain's line, before the Flood): fair skin lacking melanin, thin straight light hair (the Bishop, "We Are Born in Babylon"). NOT leprous, not Black.
- Sex matches the label (M/F).

Dress and head (labels show the role: King, Queen, Pharaoh, High Priest, Priest ...)
- King / Queen / Emperor (and the Herods): a TRUE CROWN of gold with raised points and precious stones. FAIL on a plain band, circlet or diadem. Queens: the crown sits over a cloth head covering, NO HAIR showing.
- Pharaoh: shaven BALD head, Egyptian eyeliner, draped fine white linen. NO crown, NO Egyptian headdress (nemes), NO beaded collar, nothing like a modern T-shirt.
- High Priest: a wound white linen mitre (NOT a pointed bishop's mitre) with a small gold plate on the front, the blue robe, the breastplate of stones.
- Priest (tribe of Levi): white linen garments and a linen bonnet.
- Every other man: BARE head (no crown, band, hat or turban).
- Women: a head covering (Israelite and Hebrew women must have one), modest dress.
- Israelite men from the Wilderness on: fringes with blue on the garment borders.
- Ancient dress only: nothing modern, no national costume of today (no kimono, no sari, no Chinese robes).
- Joseph: shaven, thirty, draped fine linen with a gold chain about his neck (Genesis 41:14, 42, 46); not a modern shirt.
- Cain: FULLY leprous, melanin gone: whole skin white as snow, BLUE eyes, straight BLOND hair and beard (Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 13:13; the assembly). Dignified, no sores.
- Adam and Eve: ROYAL garments (rich royal robes), no crown; Eve with her head covered. FAIL on animal skins, fur, or plain peasant dress.
