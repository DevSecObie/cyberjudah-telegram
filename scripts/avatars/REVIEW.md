# Review rules for every portrait and Timeline picture (from the KJV, the Apocrypha and the assembly's teaching)

An image PASSES only if ALL that apply to it hold. When in doubt, FAIL and regenerate. Scripture takes
precedence over any rule below that conflicts with it; where the interpretation is unresolved, the
image is marked UNRESOLVED and the conflict is named, never decided here. Each rule's grounds (explicit
scripture, the assembly's interpretation, or the owner's artistic direction) are in docs/AVATARS.md,
section 9 (direction of 2 October 2026).

Frame
- One person, head and shoulders, painted; face whole and centred (a circle crop keeps the whole face).
- Square full-bleed slate-blue background to the edges. A thin light frame is OK (trimmed). FAIL: painted inside a circle/oval, dark or ornate frame, white page.
- No text, letters, numbers, watermark. No halo, glow behind the head, light rays, cross, star, horns, wings, weapons, armour. No nudity, nothing grotesque, deformed or comic. Calm, dignified.
- EVERY FACE MUST BE ITS OWN PERSON: fail if a face looks like the same man or woman as another tile, or as any approved portrait on the reference sheets ref-1.jpg to ref-4.jpg (read them before reviewing). Same-age elders must still differ in face shape, nose, eyes and build.

People (everyone was dark before the mingling, Genesis 2:7; only Esau's line came out red, Genesis 25:25)
- israel by tribe (the 12 Tribes breakdown): dark-skinned, with the features of that tribe's people today: Judah American Blacks; Benjamin West Indians; Levi Haitians (these three: tight woolly coils); Simeon Dominicans, Ephraim Puerto Ricans, Manasseh Cubans (tight curly hair); Zebulun Mayans, Issachar Mexicans, Reuben Seminoles, Naphtali indigenous Argentineans and Chileans, Gad North American Indians, Dan Caribs, Asher indigenous South Americans (straight thick black hair). FAIL if light-skinned or the features don't match the tribe. An Israelite with no known tribe gets no portrait (left blank).
- hebrew, adam, ham: clearly Black, dark-brown to deep-black skin, tight woolly coils. FAIL on locs/twists, straight or loose wavy hair (grey too must be tight coils), or a light face. Exception from scripture: Samson wears "the seven locks of my head" (Judges 16:13, 19). Samson is a Black man (the owner, 2 October 2026), whatever the breakdown gives Dan.
- Baldness (2 October 2026): natural baldness and a receding hairline do NOT fail ("the man whose hair is fallen off his head, he is bald; yet is he clean", Leviticus 13:40-41). What fails is a deliberately SHAVEN head ("make baldness upon their head", Leviticus 21:5; Deuteronomy 14:1), except where scripture or the brief gives it: Joseph before Pharaoh ("he shaved himself", Genesis 41:14), Pharaoh (the owner's earlier direction; Genesis 41:14 does not say the court shaved, so no one else at court is shaven on its account), Elisha ("thou bald head", 2 Kings 2:23), Samson after Delilah ("shave off the seven locks", Judges 16:19).
- Adam: in the likeness of Christ, a full thick head of WHITE woolly hair and a full white woolly beard (Revelation 1:14).
- edom (incl. Rome/Italy): fair skin with a natural ruddy flush, thin straight hair. FAIL if Black, or a sunburnt caricature.
- moab, ammon, ishmael, syria, assyria, elam, japheth: DARK-BROWN skin, and the features of their people today: moab Chinese, ammon Japanese, ishmael Arab, syria Syrian, assyria Kurdish, elam East Indian, japheth Polynesian/Pacific Islander. FAIL if the skin is light or the features don't match.
- cainline (Cain's line, before the Flood): fair skin lacking melanin, thin straight light hair (the Bishop, "We Are Born in Babylon"). NOT leprous, not Black.
- Sex matches the label (M/F).

Dress and head (labels show the role: King, Queen, Pharaoh, High Priest, Priest ...)
- King / Queen / Emperor (and the Herods): a TRUE CROWN of gold with raised points and precious stones. FAIL on a plain band, circlet or diadem. Queens: the crown sits over a cloth head covering, NO HAIR showing.
- Pharaoh: shaven BALD head, Egyptian eyeliner, draped fine white linen. NO crown, NO Egyptian headdress (nemes), NO beaded collar, nothing like a modern T-shirt.
- High Priest: a wound white linen mitre (NOT a pointed bishop's mitre) with a small gold plate on the front, the blue robe, the breastplate of stones.
- Priest (tribe of Levi): white linen garments and a linen bonnet.
- Every other man: BARE head (no crown, band, hat or turban).
- Women: a head covering (Israelite and Hebrew women must have one), modest dress.
- Israelite men, prophets and Christ included: FRINGES in the borders of the garment with a RIBBAND OF BLUE upon the fringe (Numbers 15:38; Deuteronomy 22:12 "upon the four quarters of thy vesture"; Christ's hem, Matthew 9:20). They hang from the garment's border or corners. FAIL on a blue sash, stripe or trim standing in for them. A head-and-shoulders portrait whose border is out of frame is not failed for it. Scope before Sinai (the patriarchs, Joseph, the Exodus before Numbers 15): UNRESOLVED, see docs/AVATARS.md 9.4; pre-Sinai scenes wait.
- Jewellery may be worn by men and women (2 October 2026): "both men and women… brought bracelets, and earrings, and rings, and tablets, all jewels of gold" (Exodus 35:22); Genesis 24:22, 47; 41:42; Ezekiel 16:11-12. Not a fail.
- Linen breeches (2 October 2026, the owner's direction for men): scripture names them for the priests ("linen breeches to cover their nakedness", Exodus 28:42; 39:28; Leviticus 6:10; 16:4; Ezekiel 44:18). Worn under the garment; never shown as outer dress.
- Ancient dress only: nothing modern, no national costume of today (no kimono, no sari, no Chinese robes).
- Joseph: shaven, thirty, draped fine linen with a gold chain about his neck (Genesis 41:14, 42, 46); not a modern shirt.
- Cain: FULLY leprous, melanin gone: whole skin white as snow, BLUE eyes, straight BLOND hair and beard (Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 13:13; the assembly). Dignified, no sores.
- Adam and Eve: ROYAL garments (rich royal robes), no crown; Eve with her head covered. FAIL on animal skins, fur, or plain peasant dress.

Christ and the Most High (2 October 2026)
- Christ in his earthly ministry (corrected by the owner, 2 October 2026: "Christ had white hair in a vision in Revelation, not while he was walking around"): a man "about thirty years of age" (Luke 3:23), of Judah (Hebrews 7:14), very dark, with BLACK woolly hair and a black beard; the coat "without seam, woven from the top throughout" (John 19:23) where the scene allows; fringes with a ribband of blue on "the hem of his garment" (Matthew 9:20; 14:36). Natural face and eyes: FAIL on glowing or fiery eyes, light from the face or head. FAIL on white hair, a golden girdle about the paps, or metallic feet in an earthly scene: those are the vision.
- Christ in the vision of Revelation 1:12-16 only: "clothed with a garment down to the foot, and girt about the paps with a golden girdle"; "his head and his hairs were white like wool, as white as snow"; "his feet like unto fine brass, as if they burned in a furnace" (the assembly reads the feet as golden brown, very dark: IUIC, "The Biblical Image of Christ"). Not yet used in any picture.
- The Most High is NEVER depicted: no figure, face, silhouette, hand, light-figure or other personification. FAIL on any.

Scenes (Timeline period pictures, 640x800, cropped square on the period card and 55% x 250 on the list)
- First settle who and what the picture shows (the event, the verses, each person's nation); rules that do not apply are N/A, not passes.
- "Calm, dignified" is for portraits. A scene carries the force its verses describe (the owner, 2 October 2026: "put the proper aggression in"): Samson "bowed himself with all his might", the tables overthrown, the fire that "licked up the water", the city burnt, the lion in the arena. FAIL a scene that is gentle where the text is violent. Still no blood or gore, and no weapon the text does not give.
- Every explicit detail of the scene's verses holds: counts (eight souls, Genesis 7:13; six steps and twelve lions, 1 Kings 10:19-20; two pillars, one in each hand, Judges 16:29), objects, places, dress. FAIL on any that is wrong or added against the text.
- Each nation as the chart gives it, by the event and the record, never by whether they are the adversary. Rome is Edom (fair with a natural ruddy flush). No caricature of anyone.
- Recurring people keep their approved face: reuse the approved portrait (Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Sarah, Lot, Laban, Moses, Solomon and others in the approved set) as the reference.
- Check at full size and at the card's square crop: faces, hands (five fingers), clothing, fringes and the blue ribband, anatomy, period, consistency. Essential details must survive the crop.
